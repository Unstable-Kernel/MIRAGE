# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MIRAGE a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should demonstrate empathy, use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the project and the engineering community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publication of private information, unwelcome sexual attention, intimidation, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `kshitiz23kumar@gmail.com`. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where possible, and may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
